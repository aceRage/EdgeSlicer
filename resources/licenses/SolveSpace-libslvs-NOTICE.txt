SolveSpace libslvs (geometric constraint solver)

Builds of EdgeSlicer made with the SLIC3R_CAD option (the parametric Design tab) link libslvs, the
constraint solver library of SolveSpace, statically into the application. It solves the sketch
constraints of the Design tab. Builds without SLIC3R_CAD do not contain it.
