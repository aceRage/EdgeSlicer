# EdgeSlicer 2.4.9.3

<!-- update-notice -->
**EdgeSlicer 2.4.9.3** adds Split to Fit, which cuts a large model into parts that fit your printer, and brings Bambu Studio's prime tower to the Bambu printers, with the Rib wall as the new default.
- New: Split to Fit with automatic cut planes, dowels, labelled mating faces and dowels on their own plates
- New: Bambu prime tower port (nozzle-change ramming, material blocks, H3C parity) and a "Use Bambu defaults" button on the Prime tower page
- New: experimental Curved tops (off by default, Bambu Lab X1 Carbon and Elegoo Centauri Carbon only)
- Changed: Bambu printers now default to the Rib tower wall, so two-colour towers become roughly square; towers under 100 mm are 8-12 mm shallower; old H3C projects re-slice differently; on H2D/H2C/X2D/H3C three of EdgeSlicer's own tower interface options are inactive
- Fixed: process edits lost when a project is reopened; "Invalid spacing supplied to Flow::with_spacing" on mixed-nozzle plates; Arrange, Flushing volumes dialog and H3C time estimate errors
<!-- /update-notice -->

The headline of this release is **Split to Fit** and the **Bambu prime tower port**. It follows [2.4.9.2](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.9.2-edge). Please report problems on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/issues).

## Please read

Behaviour changes that can change your G-code or what slices:
- **Old H3C projects re-slice differently** (#1056). The H3C prime tower now follows Bambu Studio 2.9.1 (side scrape, slowdown at the end of a visit, the H3C speed rules, line ironing), so an H3C project sliced before gives different tower G-code now.
- **Rib is the default tower wall on Bambu printers, H3C included** (#1084), as in Bambu Studio. Two-colour Bambu towers become roughly square instead of long. On an H2D or H2C a Benchy-size two-colour print uses about 2 g less filament and takes about 8 minutes less. Existing projects and presets that store a wall type keep it.
- **Shallower towers** (#1062). The Bambu tower follows Bambu Studio's depth table, so towers under 100 mm are 8-12 mm shallower.
- **Tower interface switch on H2D, H2C, X2D and H3C** (#1062). The Bambu tower interface switch is on in the preset. While it is on, three of EdgeSlicer's own interface options are inactive.
- **Mixed nozzle sizes on the H3C rack share one merged prime tower** (#1072). This is a change to how the tower is laid out and it has not yet been printed on hardware with mixed nozzle sets. Treat the first print of such a plate with care.
- **Curved tops stay off** unless you turn them on (#1021); see New.

## New

- **Split to Fit** (#1065, #1074, #1079, #1083, #1086). Cuts a large model into parts that fit the build volume of the current printer: cut planes are found automatically and can be dragged in the 3D view, dowels (pins) are added automatically and you click to add or remove them, loose pieces are separated and labelled, mating cut faces get matching recessed labels, parts can be laid on a cut face, and dowels go on plates of their own.
- **Bambu prime tower port** (#1049, #1052, #1056, #1062, #1063, #1072, #1073, #1084). The Type 1 tower follows Bambu Studio 2.9.1 and OrcaSlicer: nozzle-change ramming with post-extrusion cooling, material blocks planned by adhesiveness category, H3C parity, the contact-layer interface with the firmware purge, adhesiveness categories for filaments that have none, a cool-down during the tower and a first-layer flow ratio. On H2D/H2C/H3C/X2D the extruder-change retraction (M620.11 K/R) now follows the outgoing filament.
- **Use Bambu defaults** button (#1080) under Enable on Process > Multimaterial > Prime tower. On Bambu printers it copies the tower settings from the Bambu system process the preset inherits from. It is greyed out while a nozzle's own process is shown.
- **Merged prime tower for mixed nozzle sizes on the H3C rack** (#1072). A plate that mixes nozzle sizes (for example 0.4 and 0.8) prints one prime tower planned as a ladder per nozzle, as Bambu Studio does. It has not yet been printed on hardware with mixed nozzle sets.
- **Curved tops** (#1021, #1082), experimental and off by default (Quality > Z contouring). The last top layers of gently curved surfaces follow the surface instead of stepping. It works only on the Bambu Lab X1 Carbon and the Elegoo Centauri Carbon; on other printers it prints flat and shows a notice. A head collision check blocks the export when the head would hit the part.
- **Head clearance model and Z contouring head collision warning** (#1004). Z contouring warns when the print head would collide with the part.
- **Support interface temperature** (#1051). A new support setting prints the interface filament at its own temperature on every layer, including the first, with a warning when it clashes with the filament in use.
- **Ending sound** (#1043). For Bambu printers whose end G-code plays a tune, an Ending sound button turns a MIDI file into the end tune.
- **Vendor connectors** (#1085) can download through POST endpoints and through endpoints that answer with the file itself. Placeholders a connector does not know are refused on save.
- **Assembly tool** (#1033). The Parallel distance and Rotate around center boxes are back, and Auto-fit keeps both picks.
- **Per-nozzle process on the H3C** (#1048, #1050). The selector now appears on a rack of one size, and pinned rack slots and the TPU285 slot get a process of their own.
- **Polymaker H3C filament presets** (#1045). 68 new @BBL H3C presets for Polymaker filaments. They are made by EdgeSlicer from the H2C set and are not published by Polymaker.
- **Tower layering check** (#775). A prime tower is only checked for equal layer heights when the plate actually prints one.

## Fixes

- **Project process edits lost on re-open** (#1064). Changes made to a system process came back as the preset values every time a project was opened again, on every printer.
- **Stale purge-row position on merged tower visits** (#1073). The first purge row after a tool change could lose its X position and push a whole row of filament out in one spot.
- **"Invalid spacing supplied to Flow::with_spacing"** (#1072). Painted regions on mixed-nozzle plates kept the wrong nozzle's line widths.
- **H3C time estimate and flush** (#1042). Rack hotends that already hold the next filament are no longer booked with unload and load time or asked to flush, so the estimate for many-filament projects is much shorter. Pre-heat, pre-cool and prime volume follow the same rule.
- **H3C remaining filament changes** (#1046). The printer screen now gets the M73 E counters, as from Bambu Studio.
- **Arrange** (#999, #1000, #1060, #1061). An object beside the prime tower is no longer sent past the plate limit, results are written to the right objects, auto-rotate keeps the object's own turn, and arranged objects stay off bed exclusion zones. A new Arrange option, Avoid nozzle reach zones (off by default), keeps objects inside the area every nozzle reaches.
- **Flushing volumes dialog** (#998). Volumes no longer creep down each time OK is pressed, are no longer lost at multiplier 0, and are checked against the right filament.
- **Prime tower without sparse layers** (#1019, #1020). The first layer sits solid on the bed, the brim chamfer and bridge rows follow the layers the tower really lays, the clearance check uses the right reach for the spiral lift, and an in-place tool change no longer descends over the part.
- **Tower entry through the wall gap** (#1034). The route into the tower now enters through the gap in the wall.
- **Object labels around tool changes** (#1041). The object label is closed before a tool change on the standard tower, and tower layers are chained from the tower.
- **A1 timelapse photo** (#1014) is taken on layers above the tower's top.
- **Sliced files** (#1015). Each filament's toolhead and the wipe tower's plate entry are written correctly.

## Under the hood

- Test builds share a compiler cache across worktrees (#1053).
- The profile validator runs on a hosted runner (#1055).
- A Mac test fix for the avoid-crossing case (#1059).
- Windows link fix for the G-code editor component (#1044).
