# EdgeSlicer 2.4.6.1

<!-- update-notice -->
New in 2.4.6.1 (a hotfix for 2.4.6.0):
- Send to Bambu Connect now passes the right filaments and colours
- H2C: an AMS HT next to a second AMS is no longer missing from "Sync AMS filaments"
- Bambu camera and Stream tab: they find the printer again when discovery fails and recover after a restart
- The Windows firewall check offers to allow Public networks by default when you are on one
- macOS: the download now needs macOS 11 or later
<!-- /update-notice -->

A small fix release for [2.4.6.0](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.6.0-edge). Everything in 2.4.6.0 is included.

## Fixes

- **Send to Bambu Connect filaments and colours** (#565). Bambu Connect now shows the right filaments, colours and AMS mapping for single-nozzle printers such as the X1C, for dual-nozzle printers such as the H2D and H2C, and when sending all plates.
- **H2C and AMS HT in Sync AMS filaments** (#576). An H2C with two AMS units plus an AMS HT listed only the two AMS units, because the AMS HT shared a tray number with the second AMS and was dropped. All trays are listed now.
- **Bambu camera on the Device page** (#579). When discovery never found a Bambu printer on your network, the camera showed "Connection Failed [1:3]" on every Play. It now uses the printer's own network address from its status report, and points you to the firewall check when discovery is blocked.
- **Firewall check on a Public network** (#580). When your PC's network is filed as Public and EdgeSlicer is blocked there, the check dialog starts with "Also allow on Public networks" ticked. Untick it if you do not want that.
- **Stream tab after a restart** (#581). Bambu tiles on the Stream tab showed "not found" after the background hub restarted or was handed over by another EdgeSlicer install, until you restarted the slicer. The tab now follows the hub, with a Retry button.

## macOS

- **macOS 11 or later** is now needed for the macOS download (universal, Apple silicon and Intel); macOS 10.15 is no longer supported. The Models browser on the Home tab still needs macOS 12.
