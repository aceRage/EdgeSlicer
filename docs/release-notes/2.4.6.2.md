# EdgeSlicer 2.4.6.2

<!-- update-notice -->
New in 2.4.6.2:
- Showcase tab: presentation renders of your print with backgrounds, floors, lighting, filament finishes and your own watermark
- Viewer: X-Ray, Realistic View with shadows, centre of mass markers, new preview sliders and a quick View options button
- Support overhang highlight while painting supports, with a colour key
- Favourite process presets, like favourite filaments
- Elegoo Centauri Carbon: refreshed profiles, Upload and Print fixed, CANVAS filament sync and slot mapping, live status in the app, camera in the Stream tab
- QIDI: Box slot mapping and QIDIStudio print options when sending (new, please report issues)
<!-- /update-notice -->

The next release after [2.4.6.1](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.6.1-edge).

## New

- **Showcase tab** (#602, #609). A new tab after Preview for presentation pictures of what your printed model will look like. Pick a background (solid, gradient, image or transparent), a floor (shadow only, solid, grid, photo surfaces or a glossy floor, or keep the plate), lighting presets and a light that stays put when you orbit. Filaments get a matte, silk, glossy or transparent look, and close-ups show layer lines at your real layer height. Export stills up to 8192 px as PNG, JPEG or WebP, optionally use one as the project thumbnail, and add your own logo or text as a watermark. Scenes are saved per plate in the project file.
- **Viewer upgrades** (#587, #590, #592, #595, #601):
  - X-Ray view mode and new graphics preferences (anti-aliasing, FPS overlay).
  - Reworked preview sliders.
  - Centre of mass markers for the plate, objects and parts, including a printed-so-far marker in Preview.
  - Realistic View: smoother shading, ambient occlusion, cast shadows and optional smooth normals. Off by default.
  - A View options button next to the view cube gives quick access to X-Ray, Realistic View and Centre of Mass.
- **Support overhang highlight** (#588). While painting supports, overhangs that will and will not be supported are highlighted by layer, with a small colour key.
- **Favourite process presets** (#589). Star process presets so they are listed first, the same way favourite filaments work.
- **Elegoo Centauri Carbon** (#593, #594, #600):
  - Printer, process and filament profiles refreshed from Elegoo's own slicer, including the CANVAS filament change G-code.
  - CANVAS: read the loaded trays, sync them into your filament list, map filaments to trays at Upload and Print (remembered for next time), and a CANVAS card on the Device tab.
  - Live status in the companion app and the Printers monitor: state, progress, temperatures, fans, light and the CANVAS trays, with pause, resume and stop.
  - The printer's camera in the Stream tab (new ElegooLink camera type), sharing one connection per printer.
- **QIDI Box and print options** (#610, #611). When sending to a QIDI printer with a Box, EdgeSlicer sends the filament-to-slot mapping first and offers QIDIStudio's print options (Box, bed leveling, timelapse, polar cooler). Nothing is sent while the printer is busy. This is new and not yet tested on every QIDI model, so please report anything odd.

## Fixes

- **Elegoo Upload and Print** (#594). Upload and Print to Elegoo Link printers failed with "Start print failed"; the Elegoo upload dialog with its leveling, timelapse and plate options is back.

## Under the hood

- Command-line thumbnails and the graphics tests no longer crash when no app window exists (#614, #623).
