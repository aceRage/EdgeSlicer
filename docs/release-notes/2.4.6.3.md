# EdgeSlicer 2.4.6.3

<!-- update-notice -->
New in 2.4.6.3:
- New filament picker when sending to Elegoo, Snapmaker U1 and QIDI Box printers: colour swatches for every filament and slot
- Printers tab and app: every saved printer connection is listed, whatever printer is selected in Prepare
- No more duplicate card for an Elegoo printer that is also the selected printer
- Fixed a crash when using Merge parts in the Assembly tool
- QIDI Box: a print no longer starts on the printer's old slot mapping; EdgeSlicer reads the Box, maps every filament and asks before printing if it cannot
- Fixed a crash opening 3MF projects when the backup folder cannot be written
<!-- /update-notice -->

A fix release for [2.4.6.2](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.6.2-edge). Everything in 2.4.6.2 is included.

## New

- **Filament swatch picker for third-party printers** (#625). When you send to an Elegoo printer with a CANVAS, a Snapmaker U1 or a QIDI printer with a Box, the send dialog shows your filaments and the printer's slots as colour swatches, like the Bambu send dialog, instead of a drop-down list per filament. Slots are labelled 1A-1D (CANVAS and QIDI Box) or T1-T4 (U1), your last mapping is remembered, and a filament sent to a slot of another material asks first.

## Fixes

- **Printers tab and app device list** (#640). Saved printer connections (Elegoo Link, Moonraker, PrusaLink, Flashforge and others) were only listed while a matching printer preset was selected in Prepare. They are now always listed, like Bambu and Snapmaker printers; sending and printing are still offered only for the selected printer. An Elegoo printer that is also the selected printer no longer shows two cards, and Moonraker and PrusaLink cards no longer show pause and stop buttons that could not work there.

- **Merge parts crash** (#637). Merge parts in the Assembly tool could crash EdgeSlicer when the two objects were not picked up as selected. It now merges them, or tells you it could not and leaves your objects as they were.

- **QIDI Box slot mapping** (#643). A print sent to a QIDI printer with a Box could start on the printer's last saved slots and feed the wrong material, for example ASA for a PLA job, when the Box could not be read or a filament was not mapped. EdgeSlicer now reads the Box the way QIDIStudio does, maps every filament the job uses, checks the mapping back on the printer, and offers Upload only (or asks before printing) when it cannot. A filament sent to a slot of another material asks first, for every printer that reports its slots. Sends from the phone app and the Stream tab follow the same rules. QIDI print options (Box, leveling, timelapse, polar cooler) are shown even before the printer answers.
- **3MF projects with an unwritable backup folder** (#608). Opening or importing a 3MF no longer crashes when the backup folder cannot be created; it falls back to the system temp folder.
- **macOS background web server** (#639). Fixed a crash when the built-in web server stopped right after a request.

## Under the hood

- Printer MQTT certificates and passwords are no longer written to the log (#527).
- Test-suite stability fixes (#641, #651).
