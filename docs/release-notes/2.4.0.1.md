# EdgeSlicer 2.4.0.1

<!-- update-notice -->
2.4.0.1 fixes prime tower adhesion on Bambu Lab printers, especially the dual-nozzle H2D and H2C.

Fixes:
- Prime tower with "No sparse layers": the tower's outer wall was printed at travel speed on every layer, the first layer included, which is why these towers kept coming loose. It now prints at normal tower speed.
- Bambu Lab process presets with an "auto" tower brim now get the brim Bambu Studio prints (it grows with tower height, up to 8 mm) instead of no brim at all.
<!-- /update-notice -->

Everything in [2.4.0.0](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.0.0-edge) is included.

## Fixes

- **No sparse layers on Bambu Lab printers.** After a filament change, the move back down to the compacted tower was a bare fast Z move, and the tower wall that followed inherited its speed (1000 mm/s), including on the bed. It is now retract, Z move, unretract, as in Bambu Studio, and the tower stays at its normal speed. Tower heights were already right in 2.4.0.0 and are unchanged.
- **Auto tower brim.** The H2D, H2C, X2D, H2S, P2S and A2L process presets set the prime tower brim to "auto" (-1), which the Bambu Lab tower generator turned into no brim. It now prints height / 100 × 8 mm, capped at 8 mm, as Bambu Studio does and as the preview already showed. Presets with an explicit brim width are unchanged.
- **Tower brim speed.** Brim loops carry their own speed rather than inheriting the previous travel speed.

These changes only affect Bambu Lab printers with a prime tower: the tower speed fix applies when "No sparse layers" is on, the brim when the brim width is "auto". Everything else slices exactly as in 2.4.0.0.
